# Letter to a pilot department office (NOT SENT)

One letter per office. Take the office's address and phone from its own official website or directory, never from an intermediary. Fill every `____`. This is a request for information and a nomination route; it does not ask for approval of the platform or imply government endorsement.

To: The Head of Office, ____ (department, mandal or district office)
____ (official address)

Date: ____

Subject: Request for the office's official email domain and a verification contact, for a civic information platform pilot

Sir or Madam,

We are ____ (entity name and status, or "a non-profit now being formed, hosted by ____"). We run a non-partisan civic participation platform in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, piloting in ____ (mandals). Residents report local issues, and verified officers of the concerned department can see and respond to the issues that relate to their area. The platform is independent of any political party and does not act for the government.

To let the right officers take part, and only genuine officers, we ask you for:
1. The official email domain or domains your office's staff use (for example the exact part after the @ sign).
2. The telephone number of the office, from the department's official directory, that our administrators may call to confirm that a named person holds a post. We never use a number given on an applicant's letter.
3. Whether the office wishes to nominate officers to take part, and the post, not personal details, that should hold the role.

Officers who apply will give their name, post, department, an official email address and a photo or scan of an official letter confirming the post. Two of our administrators check the letter and confirm the posting by calling the office directory number. Applicants' details are used only for this purpose and kept as set out in the notice they see before applying; a copy of the notice is enclosed (____).

Participation is voluntary, and nothing in the platform commits your office to any action or time limit.

Yours faithfully,
____ (name, role, entity, monitored email on the entity's domain, phone)
