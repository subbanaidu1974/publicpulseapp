Subject: Information requested under the Right to Information Act, 2005

Please provide:
1. The current list of polling stations for ____ (state, or the assembly constituencies ____), in electronic spreadsheet form, giving station number, name, assembly constituency number, part number and the building or landmark.

3. Any conditions on reuse of this list, and whether it may be reused with attribution to your office.

I do not request any voter-level data or electoral roll.
