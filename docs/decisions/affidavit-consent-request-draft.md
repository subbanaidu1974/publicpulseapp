# Draft: written-consent request to ADR (NOT SENT)

To: Association for Democratic Reforms (ADR) / MyNeta team
Subject: Request for written permission: use of MyNeta candidate data in a non-partisan civic information platform (Andhra Pradesh and Telangana)

Dear ADR team,

We are building a non-partisan civic information platform for voters in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, available in English and Telugu. It shows election results from public sources and follows the Model Code of Conduct: every candidate gets the same page layout, and we do not rank or score candidates.

At present our candidate pages only link out to MyNeta and the ECI affidavit portal. We copy and store nothing from MyNeta.

We would like your written permission to consider the following (please strike out anything you do not allow):
1. Showing selected fields (for example declared assets, declared cases, education) for each candidate on our page, with attribution to ADR/MyNeta and a link to the source page.
2. Periodic bulk download of the data for these two states for this purpose.
3. Any conditions on attribution, refresh timing, wording around declared cases, or takedown.

Please tell us the terms, any fee or licence, and the contact for corrections. Until we have your written reply we will continue to use link-outs only.
