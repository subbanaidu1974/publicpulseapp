## Licence register

### tcpd-nc

- Name: TCPD Lok Dhaba (Ashoka University) - free for non-commercial use with citation
- URL: https://lokdhaba.ashoka.edu.in/
- Status: restricted
- Constraints: non-commercial; citation required; do not redistribute raw TCPD files
- Attribution: Source: Trivedi Centre for Political Data (TCPD), Ashoka University, Lok Dhaba. Cite: Agarwal, Ananay et al. 2021 (see the TCPD citation page).
- Evidence: The NC term was read in the README of github.com/in-rolls/eci_results on 2026-11-22 ('free for non-commercial use with citation'). The Lok Dhaba site itself was unreachable (connection refused) from the build machine, so the exact licence variant (CC BY-NC vs BY-NC-SA) is UNVERIFIED.
- **Needs a human decision:** Is the public site non-commercial? Any ad, sponsorship, paid tier or consultancy use breaks the NC term. Counsel to confirm that publishing derived per-seat results under NC is permitted, or obtain written permission from TCPD.
