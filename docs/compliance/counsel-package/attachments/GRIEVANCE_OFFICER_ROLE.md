# Grievance Officer / Data Protection Officer: role description and appointment checklist - DRAFT

Status: **DRAFT. Nobody is appointed. Every name, address, phone and email below is a placeholder to be filled by a human.** Whether the law requires a grievance officer, a DPO, both, or only a published contact is a question for counsel (COUNSEL_PACK questions B14, C1). **[VERIFY WITH COUNSEL]**

Verified fact only: the PIB explainer on the DPDP Rules 2025 says a Data Fiduciary must display clear contact information for questions about personal data, which may be a designated officer or a Data Protection Officer, and that rights requests (access, correction, erasure) must be answered within ninety days. https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf . The IT Rules grievance-officer timelines (24 hours acknowledge, 15 days resolve) come from search summaries only and are unverified.

## Role description (proposal)
- Receives and tracks complaints and rights requests: access, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent, "I did not agree to this" reports, takedown and correction requests, moderation appeals.
- Acknowledges and resolves within the internal SLA (current draft in `grievance_officer.json`: acknowledge 3 days, resolve 30 days; counsel to align with legal minimums, which may be shorter).
- Escalates breaches to the incident commander and counsel the same day.
- Keeps a ticket log (retention row "Grievance tickets"), produces a monthly count of requests and outcomes for the public transparency page.
- Is the named contact in notices A1-A4 and on the Privacy and Terms pages.

## Qualities and independence
- Understands DPDP duties and the IT intermediary rules; reads Telugu and English; reachable by people without smartphones (phone, post, in-person route).
- Reports to the operator's board or head, not to the engineering or survey team; can escalate without being overruled on a rights request. Cannot be a political party office-bearer or a government officer whose department is the subject of reports (conflict check).
- No conflict with moderation decisions they will hear appeals on: appeals go to someone who did not decide the first time.
- Based in India (counsel to confirm whether required).
