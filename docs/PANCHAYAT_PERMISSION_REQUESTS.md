# Draft requests: permission to reuse panchayat election results (S-204)

**Status: drafts only. Nothing here has been sent.** Fill the `[PLACEHOLDERS]`, have counsel read each letter, and check the current
contact address on each Commission's own website before sending. Do not send from this file's wording alone if counsel changes
the terms you are willing to accept.

Why we ask: we have not been able to read either Commission's website or its terms of use, so we do not know whether the published
results may be reused, in what form they are available, or whether a bulk copy is allowed. We will not copy any result until the
answer is in writing, or until the terms of the site are read and clearly allow it (S-204).

What we can promise (true today, change nothing without checking): results are shown only as unit-level figures (panchayat or
territorial constituency, posts, contested, unopposed, turnout, winner); every table carries the credit line you specify and the date
we copied it; we do not alter figures; we make no ranking of villages, candidates or parties; where a party is named it is only a
party the Commission itself states backs the winner; we hold no electoral-roll or voter-level data and do not ask for any; we
correct or remove a record within [N] days of a notice from the Commission.

Which elections: the latest rounds and, if available, the one or two before them, for gram panchayat, mandal parishad (MPTC) and
zilla parishad (ZPTC). We list our understanding below only so you can correct it; it comes from news reports, not the Commission.
